Application checklist

A preparation guide for your merchant application. Use clearly fictional business, contact, and banking details for this demo.

01

Business details

  • Legal business name and trading name
  • Business type and the products or services offered
  • Registration details, where applicable
02

Contact and banking

  • A fictional contact name, email address, and phone number
  • Business address
  • Dummy bank and account details
03

Processing profile

  • Estimated monthly processing volume
  • Typical and maximum transaction amounts
  • Whether international payments are expected
